## 18. Prepared-run divergence audit

C3 had a material P1: after Setup rebuilt an unrelated parameter, the guide still foregrounded the prepared recipe and controlled-pair statement, with only a weak step-local mismatch warning and no restoration path. C3B now compares active template, recipe metadata, initialization provenance, seed, complete parameter keys/values, and applied interventions with an immutable prepared reference. Divergence changes the header to `Modified active run`, exposes active world/Noise/seed and changed context, moves focus to the warning, and states that the controlled-pair claim no longer applies.

Users may continue exploring without the claim, stage an explicit restore, cancel and retain the modified run, confirm a fresh paused tick-0 restore, or exit. Restore states exactly what runtime state is discarded and preserves separate Setup drafts and summaries. No expert control is locked and no user change is automatically undone.

## 19. Comparison-provenance audit

Availability remains generic because the existing `ortus.runComparison.v1` provenance is insufficient to declare guide ownership or a valid prepared baseline in every case. The guide shows existing label/template/seed/tick metadata for up to three summaries, offers the baseline route, and never converts availability into completion, a passed check, or a controlled-pair result.

## 20. Metric-language audit

Alignment is now described as the magnitude of the mean normalized heading vector from 0 to 1; higher means more similar model headings, not animal coordination or spatial cohesion. Dispersion is the mean world-unit distance from the current center of mass; higher means wider spread around that center, not necessarily fragmentation. The guide states that these outputs describe different bounded features and do not exhaust flock structure. Visual and numeric readings may agree or disagree; neither is called empirical evidence.

## 21. Fixed-seed language audit

The guide and records do not call this multi-seed robustness, statistical significance, empirical causality, calibration, validation, a universal threshold, or real-world coordination evidence.

## 29. Defects found

- P0: one unsafe public-handoff family. A `constructor` query key could fail inside Next's async search-parameter handling before ORTUS produced its safe error.
- P1: two families. Modified active runs could retain a materially false prepared-pair presentation; authority checks did not reject every pack/role/ownership/initialization/only-Noise/output drift.
- P2: five bounded families. Metric definitions were too vague; comparison metadata was not visible near generic availability; estimated-time and summary-capture wording was imprecise; visual/numeric and output-completeness prompts could lead interpretation; and navigation/divergence controls lacked sufficiently specific focus/readability treatment.
- P3: none recorded.

## 30. Defects fixed

The `/world` request boundary now rewrites unsafe promise/prototype-like query keys to the existing announced safe error before page search-parameter interpretation. Authority derivation now uses one strict reusable checker and fails closed under adversarial drift. Active runtime/reference comparison, visible divergence state, exact active facts, staged restore/cancel, and truthful prepared-reference labels prevent modified runs from masquerading as the pair. Generic summary provenance, exact metric semantics, estimated-time/capture language, non-leading prompts, specific navigation names, focus transfer, and compact responsive warnings resolve the bounded P2 findings.

## 31. Remaining limitations

Participant comprehension, educational outcomes, actual browser zoom, screen-reader and other assistive-technology behavior, forced colors, complete touch operation, formal WCAG review, multi-seed robustness, calibration, and empirical validity remain unverified. Existing run-summary provenance still cannot prove guide ownership or a valid prepared pair, so the UI deliberately stays generic. C3B does not add that schema or broader research workflow.
